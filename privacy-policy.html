Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 24, 2026

We Don't Track You

LocalFind does not include first-party cookies, analytics, accounts, or forms. It is a static business directory that runs in your browser.

1. Introduction

LocalFind is a static business directory. This policy describes the information displayed in the directory, the browser features used by the site, and when you leave for a third-party service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Business Information

We display publicly available business information including:

  • Business names and addresses
  • Contact information (phone numbers, email addresses)
  • Business hours and descriptions
  • Reviews and ratings
  • Payment information (UPI IDs for businesses that opt-in)

Business listing information is bundled with the public website and can be viewed by anyone who visits it.

2.2 User Information

LocalFind has no account system, submission form, or first-party analytics. It does not send search queries, browsing activity, or location data to a LocalFind backend because no such backend is used.

If you choose to use the map's location button, your browser processes the permission and location data locally to place you on the map and calculate distances.

2.3 Local Storage

LocalFind does not use localStorage. Its service worker may use the browser's Cache Storage to make app files available offline.

  • PWA Functionality: Service worker cache for offline access

Note: LocalFind does not use first-party cookies for tracking, analytics, or advertising.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our service
  • To display business listings and contact information
  • To enable offline access via PWA (Progressive Web App)

We do NOT:

  • Track you across websites
  • Use analytics or advertising cookies
  • Sell your data to third parties
  • Profile you for marketing purposes

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

LocalFind does not operate a first-party user-data service. Your browser can connect directly to third parties when the site loads their resources or when you choose an external link.

  • Business Contact: When you click a phone number or WhatsApp link, you're directly contacting the business (we don't intercept this)
  • Map and interface resources: The map, fonts, icons, and QR code library are loaded from third-party providers, which may process standard connection data under their own policies
  • External Links: When you click links to maps, social media, ordering services, or payment apps, you're subject to their privacy policies

LocalFind does not include a first-party analytics or advertising service.

5. Listing Corrections and Removal

Business owners can request a correction or removal of their public listing:

  • Correction: Update inaccurate business details
  • Removal: Request that a listing be removed from a future site release

Use the contact details below to make a request.

6. Data Security

LocalFind is served over HTTPS in production and uses a content security policy to limit script and resource origins. Business listing data is public and should not contain private information.

  • HTTPS encryption for data transmission
  • Content security policy and browser security headers where hosting supports them
  • No first-party user accounts or private user-data store

7. Data Retention

Business listings remain in the published site until they are changed or removed in a later release. Browser caches may retain previously visited app files until the cache is updated or cleared.

8. Third-Party Links

Our platform contains links to external websites (Google Maps, WhatsApp, social media, etc.). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party sites. Please review their privacy policies.

9. Children's Privacy

LocalFind does not create accounts or knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of LocalFind after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. International Data Transfers

Third-party resource providers and external links may process connection data in other countries. Review their privacy policies before using those services.
